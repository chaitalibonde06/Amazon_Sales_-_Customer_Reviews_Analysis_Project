# Amazon_Sales_-_Customer_Reviews_Analysis_Project 

This project analyzes Amazon product sales, pricing, discounts, ratings and customer reviews to find top-performing products, customer preferences and pricing patterns. I cleaned the Kaggle Amazon Sales Dataset in Python (1,465 rows reduced to 1,351 unique products), created new features such as price range and estimated sales, answered 10 business questions in MySQL, and completed 10 exploratory analysis tasks with 10 visualizations in Pandas, Matplotlib and Seaborn.

The notebook also generates a cleaned dataset, statistical summary, business insights, final recommendations and a 9-page PDF report automatically. Finally, I built a 6-page interactive Power BI dashboard with 6 KPI cards and synced slicers. Key findings: Electronics brings about 82% of the estimated revenue, the average rating is 4.09, the average discount is 46.7%, and deeper discounts do not lead to higher sales.
